FAQ

FAQ

Clear answers before you enroll. Final service terms and disclosures are always provided in writing before service begins.

How long does the process usually take?

Many clients stay active for 3 to 6 months, depending on the file and how bureaus or furnishers respond. Initial review is typically completed within a few business days after the needed documents are received, and response cycles often take 30 to 45 days.

Can you guarantee results?

No. We do not guarantee deletions, score increases, approvals, or exact timelines. Results vary based on your credit profile, account history, and how bureaus or furnishers respond.

What do I need to get started?

Most clients need a government-issued ID, proof of address, and current 3-bureau credit report access. If you already have prior dispute letters, bureau responses, or collector correspondence, those are helpful to include too.

Will I get updates?

Yes. Updates are posted as the file moves, and clients can track progress through a private portal. Active monthly plans also include communication support tied to the level of service you choose.

Do I still have to pay my existing bills?

Yes. You should continue making payments on valid obligations. Credit repair support does not replace your payment responsibilities to lenders or creditors.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation rights and instructions are included in your signed agreement and required disclosures before service begins. Billing and cancellation terms are provided in writing before enrollment.
